# Slop catalogue

Check the final draft against both lists. One instant-fail hit, or two soft-fail
hits, means rewrite that piece (not just swap the words).

## Instant fail

English
- "In today's fast-paced world", "In the ever-evolving landscape of"
- "Unlock the power of", "Take your X to the next level", "game-changer", "revolutionize"
- "Elevate your", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "state-of-the-art", "world-class", "best-in-class"
- "Delve", "tapestry", "navigate the complexities"
- "Are you tired of ...?" as an opener
- Any claim with a number, name or result that has no proof behind it

Indonesian
- "Di era digital yang serba cepat ini", "Di tengah persaingan yang semakin ketat"
- "Solusi terbaik untuk Anda", "terdepan", "kelas dunia", "revolusioner", "inovatif" with nothing after it
- "Tingkatkan bisnis Anda ke level selanjutnya"
- Any klaim with an angka, nama or hasil that has no proof

## Soft fail

- Three adjectives in a row; "amazing", "incredible", "powerful", "innovative", "luar biasa", "canggih"
- Exclamation marks in more than one sentence
- A question as the first line and its obvious answer as the second
- "Whether you're X or Y" / "Baik Anda X maupun Y"
- Emoji or emoticons (plain text only)
- A closing line that repeats the opening
- "We are passionate about", "Kami berkomitmen untuk" with no concrete example
- Passive hedging: "can help you to potentially"

## Instead

Name the product, the person it is for, what changes for them, and the next step.
One concrete detail beats three adjectives. If the detail is not in the business
context, ask for it or write `[NEED: detail]`.
