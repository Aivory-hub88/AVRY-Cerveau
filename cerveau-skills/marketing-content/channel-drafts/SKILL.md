# Channel drafts

Write from one brief, shape each draft for its channel. Draft only: nothing is
posted or sent. Check the slop catalogue before handing over.

## Method

1. One purpose per piece and one call to action. Pick it first.
2. Hook in the first line, using something concrete (the problem, the number you have proof for, the offer).
3. One claim at a time; every claim has proof from the context or becomes `[NEED: proof]`.
4. Use `campaign_links` for the link; put the tagged link in the draft.

## Per channel

- **Instagram caption:** hook in the first 125 characters (the rest is folded), short paragraphs, 3 to 5 relevant hashtags at the end, one call to action. Add a one-line image or Reels idea.
- **TikTok:** a script under 30 seconds: Hook (0 to 3 s), Point (3 to 20 s), Call to action (last 5 s), with on-screen text per beat. Caption under 150 characters.
- **LinkedIn post:** a first line that earns the "see more", short lines, one insight or story, no hashtag wall (up to 3), call to action as a question or a link in the first comment.
- **WhatsApp broadcast:** under 500 characters, personal tone, one offer, one link, a clear way to opt out. Only for contacts who opted in; say so in the delivery. Never invent urgency.
- **Email campaign:** subject under 50 characters and a preview line, a single call-to-action button or link, plain greeting, a short body. Create it as a Gmail draft; sending needs approval.
- **Ad copy:** headline, body and call to action within the platform limits the user names (ask if not given); one angle per variant, give 2 to 3 variants labelled by angle.
- **Landing copy:** headline with the benefit, subheadline, three proof points (each real), one call to action, a short FAQ of real objections.

## Delivery

Give each draft under a plain label (channel, purpose, status `drafted`), then the
list of `[NEED: ...]` items and the first thing the owner should review. Save longer sets to a
new Google Doc for the owner and give the link.

## Do not

- Promise results, prices, delivery times or availability the context does not state.
- Copy a competitor's wording or name them negatively.
- Message anyone: you write the text, the owner sends it.
